Study Planner

Turn your subjects, exam dates and free hours into a day-by-day plan.

Android app

Reminders arrive even with the app closed. Free, no account, works offline — everything stays on your phone.

Download the APK Android 6.0 or newer
Android will warn you It says "unknown apps" for anything not from the Play Store. Tap Settings → Allow from this source, then Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.
  1. Tap Download, then open the file from your notifications or Downloads folder.
  2. Allow installs from your browser when asked.
  3. Open it from your app drawer and set up your subjects.

Or use it in your browser

Nothing to install and it works on iPhone too. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen like an app, offline included.

Open the web app

On Android: Chrome menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
