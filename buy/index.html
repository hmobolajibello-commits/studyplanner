I am still at school, so I cannot take payments yet. Leave your email and you will be the first to know when Pro opens. It will be a small one-off price, no subscription.

What Pro will add

  • Its own exam date per subject — a subject you have already sat stops being scheduled, and the one sitting on Friday gets the last week.
  • Priority per subject — low, normal or high, so hours shift towards what you are weakest at.
  • Mock exams — a full timed paper every week or fortnight, always for the nearest exam.
  • Backup and restore — save the whole plan to a file and load it on another phone.
The app itself stays free. Subjects, topics, links, reminders, the day-by-day plan, offline and the Android app are free for everyone and always will be. Nothing that already works will ever move behind this.

Use it now anyway

The free app is not a trial and nothing expires. The four extras above are the only things on the list.
